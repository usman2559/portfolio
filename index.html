About me

Where the interest in security started, and where it's headed.

I'm a 5th-semester BS Computer Science student at the National University of Pakistan (2024–2028), based in Rawalpindi. My focus is cyber security — specifically ethical hacking, network defence, and digital forensics — and I approach it the way most people in this field do: slowly, through consistent practice rather than shortcuts.

Day to day, that means working through vulnerable labs, reading write-ups, practicing reconnaissance and OSINT techniques, and getting comfortable with the Linux command line. I've completed structured training in ethical hacking fundamentals and a broader cyber security specialization, and I'm continuing to build toward bug bounty hunting, red team exercises, and eventually SOC analysis and incident response work.

I'm not claiming expert-level experience — I'm early in the journey and I'm upfront about that. What I can say is that I show up consistently: learning networking fundamentals, practicing with tools like Burp Suite, Nmap and Wireshark, and documenting what I learn along the way.
